Toxic Gunner now has a source-backed combined burst-and-poison DPS estimate for all five levels. Levels 0–3 use `(Damage × Burst Count) / (Cooldown + (Firerate × Burst Count)) + (Poison Damage / Tick)`; Level 4 uses `Damage / Firerate + (Poison Damage / Tick)`.
