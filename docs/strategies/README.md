# Strategies

Every strategy is tied to a mode, map, player count, game version, last-checked date, tower ownership assumptions, skill assumptions, and its evidence sources. Separate creator-demonstrated runs, community-reported builds, wiki advice, and editorial analysis. Solo coverage is required for every mode where solo play is supported; do not infer solo viability from a team strategy.

The front planner has distinct Solo and Co-op views, with one attributed plan for 15 scenarios in both views: 14 entries from the current mode catalog plus Hidden Wave, an optional secret extension of Fallen Mode. `scripts/test-solo-strategy-coverage.mjs` fails if a plan is removed, duplicated, or loses required attribution fields, or if the declared catalog drifts. Co-op plans are explicitly labeled editorial and not independently tested unless the row says otherwise. Their role suggestions are grounded in mode-specific wiki mechanics, not presented as verified clears. Hidden Wave's solo plan adapts the Official Wiki trigger and wave-40 boss instructions and is explicitly unverified as a solo triumph. The planner displays the v2.11.0 snapshot and its review date, linked to the official Roblox listing; creator and community runs retain their own report dates and limits. Most plans are conservative checklists until a complete, version-stamped route is independently verified. Frost, Molten, Fallen, and Pizza Party have linked creator solo-guide reports; a Frost community poster also reports a win after receiving advice, while replies disagree about loadouts and detection. Badlands II, Pizza Party, Molten, Fallen, and Polluted Wasteland II also have solo triumph badges listed by the Official Wiki. Hardcore and Voidcore community reports remain anecdotal, with Voidcore reports explicitly marked as conflicting. Polluted Wasteland II has official evidence of a solo triumph, but no reproduced route here. See [solo strategy evidence](../research/solo-strategy-evidence.md).
