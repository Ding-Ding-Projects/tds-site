# Strategies

- [Solo playbook](solo-playbook.md): mode-by-mode Solo decisions and confidence labels.

The front planner has distinct Solo and Co-op views, with one attributed plan for each of the 15 scenarios in both views. Every Solo view adds four practical decision checkpoints covering an opening, a mode-specific midgame problem, a late-game or boss response, and evidence interpretation. The Frost Solo plan separately identifies the Wave 33 Frost Champion mini-boss and Wave 40 Frost Spirit final boss, with source-backed hidden/lead detection, firerate-debuff, beacon-priority, and late hidden-phase notes. `scripts/test-solo-strategy-coverage.mjs` fails if a plan is removed, duplicated, loses required attribution, if the declared catalog drifts, or if any mode loses one of its four Solo checkpoints or exposes the checklist in Co-op. It also checks source attribution and the critical Frost encounter facts. Steps distinguish mode facts, creator or community reports, and editorial advice. They support planning and are not verified wave-by-wave clears. Co-op entries remain explicitly labeled editorial and not independently tested unless the row says otherwise. Hidden Wave adapts the Official Wiki trigger and wave-40 boss instructions and remains explicitly unverified as a solo triumph. The planner displays the v2.11.0 snapshot and its review date, linked to the official Roblox listing; creator and community reports keep their dates and limits. Frost, Molten, Fallen, and Pizza Party have linked creator solo-guide reports; a Frost community poster also reports a win after advice, while replies disagree about loadouts and detection. The Official Wiki Index lists solo triumph badges for several modes, but its Polluted Wasteland II solo badge conflicts with the mode page statement that solo completion is impossible. Pizza Party extra defense applies only to Lost Souls matches. See [solo strategy evidence](../research/solo-strategy-evidence.md).


The Solo and Co-op planner uses mode-aware map choices. Pizza Party, Badlands II, and Polluted Wasteland II stay on their named maps; Hidden Wave offers the long-map options referenced by its strategy notes; rotating or chapter-based modes use current-trial, current-arena, or current-chapter labels. Other modes use the planner's general map list. Changing modes replaces an invalid prior selection automatically. A selected map changes the displayed assumption only: placement geometry has not been verified.
