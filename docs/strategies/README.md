# Strategies

Every strategy is tied to a mode, map, player count, game version, last-checked date, tower ownership assumptions, skill assumptions, and its evidence sources. Separate creator-demonstrated runs, community-reported builds, wiki advice, and editorial analysis. Solo coverage is required for every mode where solo play is supported; do not infer solo viability from a team strategy.

The front planner has distinct Solo and Co-op views, with one attributed plan for each of the 15 scenarios in both views. `scripts/test-solo-strategy-coverage.mjs` fails if a plan is removed, duplicated, or loses required attribution fields, or if the declared catalog drifts. Co-op plans are explicitly labeled editorial and not independently tested unless the row says otherwise. Hidden Wave's solo plan adapts the Official Wiki trigger and wave-40 boss instructions and is explicitly unverified as a solo triumph. The planner displays the v2.11.0 snapshot and its review date, linked to the official Roblox listing; creator and community runs retain their own report dates and limits. Most plans are conservative checklists until a complete, version-stamped route is independently verified. Frost, Molten, Fallen, and Pizza Party have linked creator solo-guide reports; a Frost community poster also reports a win after receiving advice, while replies disagree about loadouts and detection. The Official Wiki Index lists solo triumph badges for several modes, but its Polluted Wasteland II solo badge conflicts with that mode page's statement that solo completion is impossible. The Pizza Party extra-defense modifier applies to Lost Souls matches and stays scoped there. See [solo strategy evidence](../research/solo-strategy-evidence.md).

The Solo and Co-op planner uses mode-aware map choices. Pizza Party, Badlands II, and Polluted Wasteland II stay on their named maps; Hidden Wave offers the long-map options referenced by its strategy notes; rotating or chapter-based modes use current-trial, current-arena, or current-chapter labels. Other modes use the planner's general map list. Changing modes replaces an invalid prior selection automatically. A selected map changes the displayed assumption only: placement geometry has not been verified.
