## Evidence labels

- **Mode fact** means the linked current mode or enemy source documents a rule or wave detail.
- **Creator report** means a public guide claims a Solo clear. It does not prove the route still works after balance changes.
- **Community report** means an individual outcome or loadout claim. Keep missing wave logs, levels, and map details visible.
- **Editorial checklist** means a practical prompt derived from the stated rules. It is not a tested route.

When recording a reproduced run, include player count, game update, mode, map, tower levels and skins, skills, consumables, and the wave-by-wave purchase or sale sequence. Preserve failed attempts too, with the first wave where the plan stopped working. See [source research and disagreements](../research/solo-strategy-evidence.md).
