# Tower Defense Simulator deep-research prompt

Copy the prompt below into ChatGPT Deep Research. Treat the result as a dated research report, not a substitute for checking current in-game rules.

```text
Research Roblox Tower Defense Simulator (TDS) comprehensively using current primary sources and dated community evidence. State the research cutoff date and the exact game version. Browse the web broadly, but prioritize the Roblox experience, Paradoxum Games announcements, and the official TDS Wiki. Use creator videos, public discussions, and community guides only as secondary evidence. Cite every changing or disputed claim with a direct source link and publication or update date. Separate verified facts, source-reported claims, inference, and unknowns. Do not invent stats or smooth over contradictions.

Cover:
1. All towers: unlock and placement costs, every upgrade level, damage, attack timing, range, placement limits, targeting, hidden/lead/flying detection, abilities, buffs, debuffs, special firing cycles, and documented patch changes. Explain each DPS formula and its omissions.
2. All enemies and bosses: HP, speed, armor/defense, wave appearances, traits, resistances, immunities, abilities, summons, and counters. Flag contradictory or missing values.
3. Every mode and challenge: entry requirements, player-count rules, wave counts, rewards, income rules, modifiers, bosses, restrictions, and unlocks.
4. Maps: paths and lanes, placement zones, elevation, line of sight, cliffs, intersections, length, and mode-specific geometry. Link map diagrams or source pages; do not infer exact geometry from names.
5. Strategy for every mode, with a separate SOLO section and a separate co-op section. For each solo-supported mode, seek a reproducible solo build order by wave, exact map, tower loadout and ownership requirements, skill tree, consumables, target priorities, upgrade timing, cash/economy assumptions, boss plan, and failure recovery. Distinguish a proven clear from a general checklist. Treat solo feasibility as a different question from whether somebody posted a solo claim. Investigate disputed or possibly unsupported solo modes without asserting that a mode is soloable or impossible unless current evidence supports it.
6. Beginner progression, currencies, missions, events, skill trees, matchmaking/PvP, and current official announcements.
7. A searchable, source-linked data schema for towers, enemies, modes, maps, wave tables, and strategies. Include page revision or update date, URL, license/reuse status where relevant, and confidence for each record.

Deliver: (a) an executive summary, (b) a coverage matrix with gaps, (c) normalized tables for towers, enemies, modes, maps, and solo strategies, (d) worked calculations with units, (e) strategy comparisons by mode and player count, (f) a conflict log showing each competing claim and its source, (g) a list of unresolved questions, and (h) a change-monitoring plan. For community strategies, preserve the author, date, map, player count, loadout, restrictions, game version, and whether a full run or wave log is actually shown. Do not reproduce long passages or unlicensed images. Do not copy a wiki wholesale; summarize and attribute. Clearly label any recommendation that is your inference, and never describe an unverified build as guaranteed.
```
